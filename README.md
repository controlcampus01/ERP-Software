## About Control Campus

Control Campus is a **School ERP Software in India** designed for schools, colleges and educational institutions that want to manage their daily operations digitally.

Educational institutions handle a large amount of information every academic year. Student profiles, attendance records, examination marks, fee details, staff information and parent communication are often managed by different departments.

Control Campus provides a centralized system that connects these activities and gives authorized users access to the information required for their roles.

The platform is designed to support school administrators, teachers, accountants, staff, students and parents through dedicated management functions.
